Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our cryptographic browser-first approach.

Are my screenshots uploaded to any server? expand_more
No. Never. Redaktix executes all machine vision, text parsing, and pixel rendering entirely on your machine using WebAssembly and Web Workers. Inspect your browser Network tab to verify zero network requests are dispatched when uploading or editing.
Can Redaktix read or train AI models on my screenshots? expand_more
No. Because the images never reach any external infrastructure, we have zero access to your pixels or data. We cannot train models on your data, sell it, or accidentally expose it in a database breach.
Is blackout safer than blur? expand_more
Yes. While Redaktix offers heavy pixelation and blur options, solid blackout is the gold standard for security. Modern neural networks can sometimes invert shallow blurs, but solid black permanently destroys the underlying pixel data forever.
Does it work directly with copied screenshots? expand_more
Yes! You do not need to save images to your desktop. Simply press Cmd+Shift+4 or Win+Shift+S, switch to Redaktix, and hit Cmd+V.
Which image formats are supported? expand_more
Redaktix supports PNG, JPG, JPEG, and WebP images. All outputs are exported as standard, lossless PNG files ready for pasting directly into chat or documentation tools.
Can teams create automatic privacy rules? expand_more
Yes, on the Team plan you can define organizational presets: automatically mandate blackout for all Stripe keys, mask company-specific email domains, or require confirmation before downloading any image containing unmasked IP addresses.
Does Redaktix guarantee every sensitive item will be detected? expand_more
Automatic detection is an intelligent assistant designed to accelerate your workflow and catch common leaks. However, you always retain full manual control: you can easily drag custom redaction boxes over any unusual or bespoke identifiers before exporting.

Privacy Policy

Redaktix is 100% client-side and local-first. Screenshots, uploaded files, images, and OCR text are processed inside your browser. They do not leave the tab, and they are not sent to a Redaktix server or any other server.
